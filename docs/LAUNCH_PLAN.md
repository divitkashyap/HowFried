# HowFried launch plan

Status: draft for review. Nothing posted. Start with a private developer preview;
record the launch video together when the owner returns. No scheduled posting or
outreach is authorized by this document.

## The idea people should repeat

**A tiny dog that reminds you to touch grass while your agents work.**

HowFried is the product. **Pip** is the recommended working name for the puppy:
short, warm, easy to caption, and apt for a very small companion. Alternatives:
**Pippin** (storybook), **Nori** (soft), **Biscuit** (obvious and friendly), **Poppy**
(the owner's suggestion). These are creative candidates, not cleared trademarks or
verified available handles. Keep the current app name until a name is chosen.

Voice: affectionately ridiculous, calmly useful. The dog is your friend, not your
manager. Cute visuals earn attention; a working, respectful reminder earns trust.

Lines to test:
- “your agents have tasks. your dog has concerns.”
- “one more prompt, you said. 47 prompts ago.” (comedic caption, not a product metric)
- “token maxxing. tiny dog intervention.”
- “five minutes outside. supervised by someone very small.”
- “your tokens can wait. Pip cannot.”

Avoid accusing AI companies of deliberately preventing sleep, claiming proven brain
harm, or pretending token counts measure productivity. We can tease the habit
without inventing motives or medical benefits. Do not promise virality.

## Audience: start narrower than everyone

1. **Mac developers using Claude Code or Codex daily.** Their problem is the endless
   prompt/check/re-prompt cycle. They understand the joke and can test hooks.
2. **Indie builders and design engineers on X.** They share playful desktop craft:
   the notch paws and tiny walk are the hook; the native app is the proof.
3. **Desk workers and cozy productivity viewers on TikTok/Reels.** Broader emotional
   fit, but the present app does not observe every chat website. Invite interest,
   disclose Mac/developer-tool scope, and use responses to prioritize later adapters.

Launch the actual product to group 1, show the charm to group 2, learn from group 3.
Do not advertise Windows, Linux, ChatGPT web, universal token tracking, or Spotify.

## What we can show now

Working prototype: configurable prompt threshold or elapsed session time, notch
footsteps, puppy entrance, five-minute park, skip/snooze, dark UI, local-clock moon,
and opt-in local piano. Claude token observation is optional and partial. Agents
continue underneath; the app does not stop their work or enforce a spend limit.

Before inviting installations: verify one real submission in each supported client,
the default 20-prompt trigger, and global dismissal from another app. Complete the
existing manual checks. The current ad-hoc build is not a notarized download;
choose a tested distribution route before claiming “download and go.” Until then,
CTA is **follow for the Mac beta** or **developer preview**, not “available for everyone.”

## Hero video: 24 seconds, one joke, one product moment

Make the production polished through framing, sound and restraint. Preserve the
real puppy animation. No cinematic logo intro or simulated functionality.

| Time | Picture | Caption / sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Tight crop: a paw appears beside the physical notch | “i gave my AI addiction a tiny supervisor” |
| 2–5s | Alternating paws cross; a short synthetic coding prompt behind it | “he has concerns.” Two soft paw taps. |
| 5–10s | Puppy trots in; pull back smoothly to show the park | Let the walk breathe. Optional quiet bell. |
| 10–14s | Puppy sits at its matched small size | “five minutes. go be a person.” |
| 14–18s | Brief close-up of Skip/Snooze and optional Play | “your agents can keep working.” |
| 18–21s | Night moon variation, tiny puppy in the frame | “a break buddy for people who keep saying one more prompt.” |
| 21–24s | Simple end card with puppy | “HowFried · Mac beta soon” / “follow to meet Pip” after naming decision |

Label compressed waiting/countdown footage “preview · timing shortened.” Do not
show “I'm back” recaps, automatic playlist selection or git stats as working features.
The existing recordings are composition references, not final masters.

Production session, bounded to 90 minutes:
- 0–15: synthetic desktop, hide personal UI, choose caption and working name.
- 15–35: record native app at full display resolution, ideally 60fps; capture warning,
  whole entrance, sitting hold, controls, and day/night separately. No real chats.
- 35–65: edit one master with gentle punch-ins, large readable captions, warm palette.
- 65–80: mix licensed/original music and paw sounds; export and inspect on a phone.
- 80–90: alternate first two seconds and final CTA, then stop. No endless polishing.

Deliverables: 1080×1350 X cut, 1080×1920 TikTok/Reels cut, 1920×1080 archive master,
clean thumbnail, caption text, and a silent-readable version. Recompose vertical
shots rather than shrinking the whole desktop. Keep key text clear of platform UI;
check the actual upload preview before publishing. Master audio must be original or
licensed for cross-platform use. No paid generation needed for this first launch.

## Release sequence

- **Before launch:** finish real hook checks; recruit 5–10 willing Mac developer testers
  through owner-approved invitations. Observe setup friction and whether breaks help.
- **Launch day:** one X hero video, stay around to answer questions, clear beta status.
  Post the recomposed vertical cut on TikTok/Reels when ready; no automatic cross-posts.
- **Next day:** share the real prototype-to-puppy progression; use the awkward first
  walking dog as a self-deprecating making-of if the owner wants. Show craft, not spam.
- **Days 3–4:** a short night-mode/mute clip or genuinely useful answer to a common reply.
- **Days 5–7:** report actual tester feedback and one improvement. Ask permission before
  quoting people. Do not manufacture testimonials, engagement or a “sold out” beta.

Use a one-week experiment, not an indefinite content treadmill. Maximum three core
creative concepts: tiny supervisor, one-more-prompt joke, cozy night companion.
No paid ads in week one. Manually compare similar posting windows, recognizing that
small organic samples cannot establish causation. Reuse a strong idea with a new
real feature, not repeated identical posts.

## What success means

Primary learning: can interested developers install it, get a real prompt counted,
and choose to use it again? Views alone do not answer that.

Keep a simple manual sheet: post, format, hook, impressions/views as defined by that
platform, watch-through where available, shares, qualified beta requests, successful
setups, completed voluntary breaks, day-3 return feedback. No in-app analytics SDK
needed; testers can opt in to sharing feedback. Never compare raw X and TikTok view
counts as equivalent measures.

Planning targets (not forecasts): 10 qualified testers, at least 8 successful setups,
and 5 people voluntarily using it on a third day. If views are high but setup fails,
fix onboarding before making more videos. If retention is weak, interview users
before adding pets or reward dashboards. If the first seconds lose viewers, test a
clearer puppy-first opening. Stop after the bounded week and review.
